About

Research & Background

I am a financial economist specializing in asset pricing, public finance, and the theory and history of classical liberalism.

Before joining Campbell University, I completed my Ph.D. in Finance at the University of Texas at Dallas. Before that, I graduated from The Florida State University with a Bachelor’s of Science in Economics, then worked as a public policy analyst at the DeVoe L. Moore Center at Florida State’s College of Social Sciences. In that role, I was fortunate to write about a wide variety of state, local, and national issues related to taxes, regulation, and economic liberty. I then worked as a writer and researcher for the Colloquium for the Advancement of Free Enterprise Education in the Jindal School of Management at The University of Texas at Dallas. While pursuing my doctoral studies, I was awarded the Adam Smith and Oskar Morgenstern Fellowships from the Mercatus Center at George Mason University.

I teach courses in Corporate Finance, Financial Case Studies, Commercial Banking, Advanced Investment Analysis, Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, and Econometrics at the graduate and undergraduate levels.
